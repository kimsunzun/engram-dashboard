# ADR-0112: Linked to the messaging send overhaul — shrink the group resolution source, reject sleeping recipients

- 상태: 확정 (2026-07-29, basis: user decision — the same learning Q&A session as ADR-0111, step-log 2026-07-29) · 부분 폐기 by ADR-0116 (decision 2 reject sleeping recipients) · 부분 폐기 by ADR-0121 (decision 1 the `@all` roster source — the built-in vocabulary splits into `@all`/`@here`, and `@all` also reads sleeping names)
- 관련: Amends ADR-0104 (decision 1 the runtime-registered roster source, decision 2 sleeping-recipient parking) · **ADR-0111(the send overhaul proper — this ADR is the linked amendment of what that overhaul does to the ADR-0104 clauses, read them as a pair)** · ADR-0104(decision 3 idle gate and batch flush stay unchanged) · Amended by ADR-0116 (decision 2 reject sleeping recipients) · Amended by ADR-0121 (decision 1 the `@all` roster source — the *direction* of the resolution seam survives as is, and the vocabulary that source produces grew to two)

## 맥락

ADR-0111 (reject absent recipients, multiple recipients, unify the group and individual paths) conflicts not only with ADR-0103 but also with two clauses of the supplementary ADR-0104. So that a session that reads only the old ADR does not read a discarded clause as alive, the clause amendment on the 0104 side is recorded under a separate number (the supersede tool is one new ADR per old ADR — the proper prose is canonical in ADR-0111).

## 결정

1. **Shrink the group resolution source (amends ADR-0104 decision 1):** remove the "runtime-registered roster" that was the v1 source and leave **only the built-in `@all` (everyone alive at the moment of sending, an unstored on-the-spot computation)**. Shrink @address resolution to a single point: "roster expansion → joining the multi-send of ADR-0111 decision 2". **The direction of the future folder group (`@폴더명`) itself is kept** — when folders come to be owned by the daemon, the source is re-expanded at that resolution point (do not lay down a stored-group structure in advance).
2. **A sleeping recipient = reject (amends ADR-0104 decision 2):** a sleeping recipient (process closed, session restorable) is not in the roster at the moment of sending, so it is **rejected at the entry** the same as an absent one (the parking in the old clause "park the same as absent" is replaced by a reject — a consequence of ADR-0111 decision 1). **The decision not to adopt wake itself remains valid** — but the form of revisiting it changes: the v2 candidate wake-on-request is now not "wake instead of parking" but "wake and deliver instead of rejecting".

## 거부한 대안

- **Discard 0104 in full** — decision 3 (idle gate · batch flush · delivered = real injection) is entirely valid regardless of this overhaul. It would be a wrong discard that kills a living clause, so it is rejected.
- **Bury the 0104 amendment in the proper ADR-0111 and skip the stamp on 0104** — the next session that reads only 0104 follows the registered roster and sleeping-recipient parking as valid decisions (violating the no one-way link invariant). Rejected.

## 근거

- User decision (2026-07-29): the same session as the ADR-0111 rationale section — removing the group structure (fork choice "remove — keep only @all") · reconfirming failure handling for absent/sleeping recipients.
- The original intent of ADR-0104 decision 1 (absorbing non-destructively by adding a source when folders appear) holds even if the source is only @all — the shrink does not damage the direction.

## 영향 / 불변식

- **The group management surface is removed** — the group MCP tool, the CLI subcommand, and the runtime roster store disappear (ADR-0111 decision 4). `@all` stays a reserved name that cannot be CRUDed.
- **A send to a sleeping session fails** — the sender bears the retry responsibility. To soften this, wake-on-request (a v2 candidate) must be formally revisited, and quietly reviving the parking path is forbidden.
- Decision 3 of ADR-0104 and its invariants (delivered = real injection · batch flush · oldest first · capability fallback) all survive.
