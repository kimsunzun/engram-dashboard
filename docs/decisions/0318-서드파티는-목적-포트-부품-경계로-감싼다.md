# ADR-0318: Third parties are wrapped at a purpose port and part boundary

- 상태: 제안 (2026-10-31, basis: research `docs/research/third-party-wrapping-criteria-survey-2026-10-14.md` · TRD §1-4 `store.rs` · §7 14)
- 관련: ADR-0311 · TRD §1-12 · ADR-0336

## 맥락
How do we wrap third parties such as walkdir, serde-saphyr, the tree, the table, dragging and the query?

## 결정
1. The unit of wrapping is the purpose — walkdir · std::fs → one 「라이브러리 저장소」 port (`LibraryStore`) · serde-saphyr → the format / tree, list, dragging and query → one part of ours each.
2. What is not wrapped = the language standard · foundations used everywhere (serde · tokio · React) · pure helpers used in only one place (research conclusion 3).

## 거부한 대안
- **A 1:1 wrapper per library** — the unit of wrapping is the purpose, not the library (Cockburn hexagonal — research conclusion 2 · certain) · a thin 1:1 pass-through wrapper leaks the details below it as they are, so there is no gain (research conclusion 3 · likely — conventional wisdom).

## 근거
Research conclusions 1~4 (Fowler Gateway · Cockburn hexagonal · Spolsky · Rust API Guidelines C-STABLE).

## 영향 / 불변식
- Do not export another crate's types in a public API that crosses a crate boundary (C-STABLE — research conclusion 4).
- What gets wrapped (choosing the parts) is ADR-0336; this decision fixes only the unit of wrapping.
- (Client share memo — 2026-10-31 frontend research · provisional) The tree react-arborist is tied to its own dragging (react-dnd), and the research withdrew the claim that 「트리 · 목록 · 폴더 줄이 react-dnd 관리자 하나를 나눈다」 — it takes the id to move from its own tree state, so list → tree and tree → tree moves need a drop adapter on our side (`docs/research/frontend-tree-dnd-virtualization-2026-10-31.md`). 「끌기 → 우리 부품 한 곳」 is the place for that adapter. The `@dnd-kit/core` of memo 113 cannot drive arborist — it will be decided again in the client stage.
