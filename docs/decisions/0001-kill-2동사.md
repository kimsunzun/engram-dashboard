# ADR-0001: kill = 2 verbs (shutdown + join_pump)

- 상태: 확정 (2026-06-11, verified by spike, S2/S3)
- 관련: CLAUDE.md 핵심 불변식 · `session.rs:82` · `manager.rs::kill_agent` · `transport/pty.rs`

## 맥락
The PTY process and the output pump thread must be shut down safely. We need to decide how to tie resource closure (master/child/job) to joining the pump thread.

## 결정
`session.kill` = `transport.shutdown()` **THEN** `core.join_pump(timeout)` — the two verbs are kept separate.
- shutdown: shutdown flag Release → child.kill+wait → TerminateJobObject → master.take/drop. **The master is guaranteed dropped before it returns.**
- join_pump: after that, waits up to the timeout (5s) for the pump to end.

## 거부한 대안
- **1 verb (join inside shutdown)** — the pump waits on its own termination, a self-join → hang. Joining while the master is still not released waits forever.
- **The old 6-step kill** — equivalent, but transport/core responsibilities are not separated (the form before the S10 rework). The 2 verbs fit the ownership split and are cleaner.

## 근거
shutdown drops the master → the reader gets EOF → the pump breaks → `core.finish(reason)` → done_tx. The causality is one-directional, so there is no hang. In the spike, **17~23ms** was measured from master.take to reader EOF.

## 영향 / 불변식
- **Reversing the order is forbidden** — doing join_pump before shutdown waits on a live pump and hangs.
- The terminal transition (`Killed`) is emitted by the pump alone, through finish (→ ADR-0005). The manager emits only `agent-list-updated`.
- **Premise refined by S34** (`docs/process/S34-pty-kill-hang/`, merged to master `baa97e41`; T-79): the 「근거」 chain "master drop → reader EOF → pump break, no hang" holds only while the pump keeps reading — on Windows `ClosePseudoConsole` waits for its output to be read, so after shutdown the pump keeps draining until the master is closed (S34 TRD §3A). The two-verb order is unchanged.
