# ADR-0250: The usage clock is measured as elapsed time including sleep, and only the rejection is kept on disk by wall clock

- 상태: 확정 (2026-09-27, basis: TRD §7 #6)
- 관련: TRD §7 #6 · step-log S21

## 맥락
We had to decide how to keep the clock that measures the time remaining until the usage reset. A pure wall clock can look as if it was rewound, or conversely as if it skipped ahead, when the system sleeps and wakes.

## 결정
The usage clock is measured as **elapsed time including sleep** (monotonic in nature), and **only the rejection (rate limit) state is kept on disk by wall clock** (24-hour cap).

## 거부한 대안
- **All wall clock** — a rewind (system time going backward · power saving) makes the wait time grow or shrink wrongly.
- **Not saving** — right after a restart, the address that was rejecting would be hit again, sending the request that was just rejected once more.

## 근거
TRD §7 #6.

## 영향 / 불변식
The only usage-related state persisted to disk is the rejection (rate limit), and its cap is 24 hours. Implementation commits: `7aad8f7`, `56776d6`, `c68b885`.
