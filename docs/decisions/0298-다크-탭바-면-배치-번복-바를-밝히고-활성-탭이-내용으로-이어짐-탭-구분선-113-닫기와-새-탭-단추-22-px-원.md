# ADR-0298: Dark tab bar surface layout reversed — the bar is lightened and the active tab flows into the content · tab separator 1×13 · close and new-tab buttons are 22 px circles

- 상태: 확정 (2026-10-21, 근거: user decision (on the real screen — the reason sentence the main agent presented was accepted with 「ㅇㅇ」 (decision 1) · separator 「얇게」 · text color 「업계 기준으로 추천하는대로」) + WCAG AA contrast ratio calculation + a calculation that carries the Chromium constants over by the tab head height ratio 28/34 + survey `docs/research/slot-docking-drag-ux-followups-2026-10-21.md`)
- 관련: Amends ADR-0168 (decision 5 ① "the active tab is lighter than the bar", its invariant, and the inactive tab text color, in the dark theme only) · Amends ADR-0295 (the tab separator size and the close button size in decision 1) · PRD `docs/process/S21-slot-docking/prd.md` R27 · TRD `docs/process/S21-slot-docking/trd.md` (the tab shape line of 「정해짐」) · survey `docs/research/slot-docking-drag-ux-followups-2026-10-21.md` (§4 active tab · the 「탭 색」 decision line at the end of the appendix)

## 맥락
On 2026-10-21, while working on slot docking, the user looked at the tab bar on the real screen and decided a new shape for the dark theme — the reason sentence for that shape was presented by the main agent as an example and the user accepted it with 「ㅇㅇ」 (decision 1). The trigger = the observation 「활성 탭이 안 보인다」 that came out of the user's measurement after U3 (the head ④ of the survey `docs/research/slot-docking-drag-ux-followups-2026-10-21.md`). At that time in dark, the bar was `#000000` and the active tab = the content surface `#0a0a0a`, and that difference is ΔL* ≈ 2.7 (calculation — CIE L* 0 vs 2.74 · the 「≈ 2」 in conclusion 5 of the survey was a main-agent estimate) · contrast ratio 1.06:1 (this ADR's calculation).

That dark value is not what ADR-0168 wrote. In ADR-0168 (2026-08-23), the bar = `--bg` (dark `#0a0a0a`) · active = `--bg-secondary` (dark `#141414`). The user decision of 2026-10-19 (U7c — Chrome shape) moved the tab bar to dedicated tokens — the bar `--tab-bar-bg` (dark `#000000` · light `#e3e3e3` · e-ink `#e6e6e6`) · active `--tab-active-bg` = `--bg` (the content surface). After that, ADR-0168 decision 5 ① (the active tab is lighter than the bar) survived **only as a principle** in the three themes. That ADR's token mapping (bar = `--bg` · active = `--bg-secondary`) and its e-ink caveat (「e-ink는 이 관계가 역전돼 있다」 — now in e-ink the bar `#e6e6e6` is darker than the active `#ffffff`) stopped matching that same day. ★That change was not recorded in an ADR★ — the only record is 「사용자 결정 2026-10-19」 in the tab bar comment of `src/styles/theme.css` (carried in by commit `02a7175d` U0), and the step-log 2026-10-20 U0 entry wrote 「탭바 색 ADR」 as a remaining question.

Behind the old principle is a user judgment of 2026-08-23 — when a layout where the active tab is darker than the bar was applied, it read like 「구멍처럼」 and was reverted (ADR-0168 「거부한 대안」). This decision overturns that principle and that judgment **only in the surface layout of the dark theme** — the surface layout of light and e-ink stays as it is.

In the same place the user reported that the × and + of the last tab look stuck together, and also decided the tab separator · the size of the close and new-tab buttons · the dark inactive tab text color. The light inactive text color and the hover · active behavior of the head text color were left by the user to the main agent (decisions 7 · 8). The review caught that PRD R27 · TRD still say the separator is 2×16.

## 결정
Decision 2 is a main-agent decision (internal) · 7 and 8 are main-agent decisions (user delegation — 2026-10-21 「알아서해 내가 나중에 피드백 줄게」 · **awaiting user feedback**) and the rest are user decisions (2026-10-21).

1. **Dark tab bar = bar `#242424` · active tab = content surface `#0a0a0a`** (old bar `#000000`). The active tab is darker than the bar and flows into the content below — the same shape as Chrome's active tab becoming one surface with the canvas. The surface layout of light and e-ink is not changed. Reason = the user accepted with 「ㅇㅇ」 the reason sentence the main agent presented as an example ("Zed Ayu 처럼 바를 밝게 띄우고 활성 탭이 내용으로 이어지게 하는 쪽이 이번엔 더 낫게 보였다") (2026-10-21 — it is not the user's own words).
2. **The bottom edge line of the dark bar = transparent** (main-agent decision · internal). When the bar is `#242424`, the token of that line (`--border` = `#242424`) is invisible anyway. The row height stays.
3. **Tab separator 2×16 → 1×13.** Height = the value from applying Chrome's ratio 16/34 to our 28 px head (16 × 28/34 ≈ 13) · thickness 1 = the user's 「얇게」.
4. **Put one separator after the last tab head too.** The user's report — the × and + of the last tab looked stuck together. Chrome precedent = when the last tab shows no background, the separator after it is shown (`horizontal_tab_style_views.cc` — the source line is in 「근거」).
5. **Close (×) · new-tab (+) buttons = a 22 px circle + a 13 px lucide icon · on hover, a circle background.**
   - + = Chrome `kButtonSize` 28 × 28/34 (the calculation is ≈ 23 — 22 is the user's decision).
   - × = the user's decision 22 — larger than Chrome. The visible hover shape of Chrome's close button is `kTabCloseButtonSize` 16 dp (14 with RoundedIcons · ≈ 13 by 28/34).
   - Icon 13 = Chrome `kIconSize` 16 × 28/34.
   - Hover circle = Chrome hover highlight opacity 0.16 (`--tab-button-hover-bg` = `--text` 16% · × = `tab_close_button.cc` · + = `tab_strip_color_mixer.cc` — the source lines are in 「근거」).
   - So `TAB_MIN_ACTIVE_W` = left padding 12 + name 0 + gap 6 + close circle 22 + right padding 12 = **52**. The code does not write this value by hand and calculates it from the same shared constants.
6. **Dark inactive tab text color `#888` → `#999`** — held as the per-theme token `--tab-text-inactive`. Standard = WCAG AA 4.5:1 for 12 px text. `#999` passes at 5.45:1 on the bar `#242424` and `#888` falls short at 4.38:1. After decision 8, the name · × of a hovered head are `--text`, so this gray is not placed on the hover surface. The same gray also draws the + icon on that hover circle (`--text` 16% over the bar = `#424242`) — 3.53:1 · for an icon the standard is the WCAG non-text contrast 3:1, so it passes. The user: 「업계 기준으로 추천하는대로」.
7. **Light inactive tab text color `#666` → `#636363`** (main-agent decision · user delegation). Standard = the same WCAG AA 4.5:1 as decision 6 (on the bar — that it is not placed on the hover surface is also the same as decision 6). `#666` falls short at 4.47:1 on the bar `#e3e3e3` and `#636363` passes at 4.68:1. On the hover circle of the + icon (`#c3c3c3`) it is 3.41:1, over the non-text 3:1. e-ink (`--text-muted` = `#444`) passes at 7.80 on the bar `#e6e6e6` and 5.41 on the + hover circle `#c1c1c1`, so it stays. The ADR-0168 amendment caveat in the related line, 「…다크 테마에서만」, was stamped before this decision was made and left out the light inactive text color — this ADR changes the light text color too (the stamp on the ADR-0168 side is stamped as a caveat that includes light).
8. **Head text color — the name and × share one foreground color (Chrome shape) · hover is also `--text` (our choice)** (main-agent decision · user delegation) — the name and × share one foreground color (Chrome `tab.cc`), and on hover and when active both brighten to `--text` (HEAD = only the name is `--text` when active · × is always `--text-muted`). The + icon is the same gray as the inactive ×.

## 거부한 대안
- **Keep the old dark layout (the active tab lighter than the bar · bar `#000`)** — the user looked at the real screen on 2026-10-21 and rejected it (the reason for decision 1 · trigger = 「활성 탭이 안 보인다」). Strength of rejection = **weak** (the user's visual judgment · supporting numbers = bar ↔ active surface ΔL* ≈ 2.7 (calculation — the 「≈ 2」 in conclusion 5 of the survey was a main-agent estimate) · contrast ratio 1.06:1 (calculation) — the survey found no guideline standard to compare against (survey §4)). The same visual axis (whether the active tab is darker than the bar) was decided the opposite way on 2026-08-23 (ADR-0168 「거부한 대안」 — 「구멍처럼」), and it was reversed after the user looked at the real screen on 2026-10-21.
- **The × circle at the Chrome ratio ≈ 13 px (or 18 px in between)** — after hearing that Chrome is 16 dp, the user kept 22. Strength of rejection = **weak** (user preference · no measurement).
- **Keep the inactive text colors (dark `#888` · light `#666`)** — below WCAG AA (4.5:1 for 12 px text): 4.38:1 on the dark bar · 4.47:1 on the light bar (quantitative — after decision 8 this gray is not placed on the hover surface, so only the bar is measured). e-ink `#444` passes at 7.80 on the bar, so it was not changed.

## 영향 / 불변식
- Theme tokens (`src/styles/theme.css`) — `--tab-bar-bg` (dark `#242424`) · `--tab-bar-edge` (dark transparent) · `--tab-button-hover-bg` (the button hover circle) · `--tab-text-inactive` (inactive text · dark `#999` · light `#636363` · e-ink = `--text-muted` as is).
- The button and separator constants of `src/components/layout/TabBar.tsx` (22 px circle · 13 px icon · separator 1×13 · end separator) · the head text color (the name and × one foreground color · hover · active = `--text`) · `TAB_MIN_ACTIVE_W` 52 in `src/components/layout/tabStrip/layout.ts` — it is calculated from the shared constants of the button diameter · head padding · gap, so changing those constants changes it along.
- **ADR-0168 decision 5 ① and its impact invariant (「탭 바는 활성 탭보다 어두워야 한다」) remain only as a principle in light and e-ink** — one relationship, that the active tab is lighter than the bar. The token mapping and the e-ink caveat of that ADR already stopped matching on 2026-10-19 (「맥락」 — no ADR record). In dark, do not make the bar dark again on the grounds of that invariant.
- ADR-0168's 「명도차 단독에 활성 판정을 걸지 않는다」 remains as a principle, but the channels it leans on have changed — that ADR wrote 「그중 하나를 떼면 나머지로 버티는지 다시 재야 한다」. Recalculated values: inactive separator thickness 2 → 1 px · dark active vs inactive text `#e0e0e0`:`#888` 2.69 → `#e0e0e0`:`#999` 2.16 · light `#1a1a1a`:`#666` 3.03 → `#1a1a1a`:`#636363` 2.90 · dark bar ↔ active surface ≈ 1.06 (`#000000`:`#0a0a0a`) → ≈ 1.28 (`#242424`:`#0a0a0a` — the active is darker · the direction flipped) · the always-visible × is unchanged. After decision 8, the text of an inactive head being hovered is the same `--text` as an active head, so while hovering there is no text channel — what remains is the surface shape (the round hover surface ↔ the active surface joined to the bar by wings), and the hover surface ↔ active surface (`--tab-hover-bg` vs `--tab-active-bg`) contrast is 1.06 in light · 1.52 in dark · 1.64 in e-ink. The 「dark 1.07:1」 of ADR-0168 is the value for the tokens at the time of that ADR (`#0a0a0a`:`#141414`), not the value on the screen now. ★These values are calculations and were not looked at again on the screen★.
- The 「Chrome 상수 그대로」 of ADR-0295 decision 1 is replaced by this decision for the tab separator size and the close button size (the 2×16 in A11 of the survey `docs/research/slot-docking-chrome-desktop-drag-2026-10-18.md` is Chrome's own value). Do not 「바로잡지」 the × to the Chrome ratio — it is the size the user chose after hearing the Chrome value.
- PRD R27 and the TRD (the tab shape line of 「정해짐」) were aligned to these values.
